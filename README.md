# Return-Pal

**Return-Pal** simplifies online returns—no label printing, packaging, or post-office visits required. Just schedule a pickup, and we handle everything.

---

##  Demo

Access the current live preview of the app here:  
[Return-Pal Preview on Vercel](https://return-pal-preview.vercel.app/)

---

##  How It Works

1. **Schedule a Return**  
   Easily enter your pickup details via the app's intuitive interface.

2. **Select Pickup Method**  
   Choose to leave your package at the doorstep or hand it off to a Return-Pal specialist.

3. **We’re On Our Way**  
   We'll collect your item, repackage it, handle the label, and ship it back. Expect an email with your tracking info once your parcel is dispatched.

##  Frequently Asked Questions

| Question                                                                 | Answer                                                                 |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Can I return items on weekends or holidays?                             | Yes—Return-Pal operates 24/7, including weekends and holidays.         |

| Can I return items from any online store or courier?                    | Absolutely—any online retailer and courier service supported.          |

##  Getting Started

1. Visit the [live app preview on Vercel](https://return-pal-preview.vercel.app/).
2. Navigate to **Schedule Pickup** and follow the simple, guided steps.
3. Enter your order and pickup details.
4. Choose your preferred pickup method.
5. Wait at home (or leave the item at your door) — Return-Pal takes care of the rest.
6. Receive your tracking number by email once the return is on its way.
